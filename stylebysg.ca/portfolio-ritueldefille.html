Rituel de Fille Advertisement

Editorial Illustration Concept

Objectives

Create a magazine spread using the same brand voice as a select company.

Problem

The illustration and type have to be integrated together, but some content will be lost to the gutter. A product shot is also required with a description of the product and a call to action.

How I Solved it

The gorgon took up one page of the spread, leaving the other page to fill with type, automatically solving half of the spread. To connect the two, the snakes weave through the letters. For the product shot, I opted to do a vector illustration instead as a challenge.
